1. Project Objective & Executive Summary

This quotation presents the comprehensive scope, technical architecture, infrastructure cost ownership, and commercial investment for engineering and deploying a bespoke corporate website for GPS Projects Private Limited. The platform is designed to establish an authoritative digital identity, showcase residential, commercial, and industrial construction projects, drive qualified customer inquiries with instant automated email alerts, and empower company staff with a dedicated, no-code Content Management System (CMS) to manage live portfolio listings.

2. Detailed Scope of Services & Deliverables
  • ■
    Bespoke Responsive UI/UX Architecture: Custom-built on Next.js 16 and React 19 with a mobile-first design system. Perfectly adapted for smartphones, tablets, laptops, and 4K displays, incorporating official corporate colors, high-resolution imagery, and clean typography.
  • ■
    6 Dedicated Core Corporate Pages & Sections: Homepage (hero branding, metrics counters, service previews, portfolio showcase, contact triggers), About Us (incorporation records, ROC UP II registration, mission, leadership profiles), Services (detailed breakdown of Residential, Commercial & Industrial Construction), Projects Portfolio (categorized dynamic listings), Photo Gallery (interactive lightbox viewer), and Contact Us (verified corporate address, direct phone/email actions, and inquiry form).
  • ■
    Lead Capture & Automated Email Notification System: Interactive customer inquiry form with client & server-side validation, anti-spam honeypot shielding, and automatic SMTP email dispatch forwarding inquiries directly to company management.
  • ■
    Dynamic Admin Content Management System (CMS): Secure administrative portal (/admin) empowering company staff to add, update, or archive project details, images, specifications, completion stages, and locations without writing code.
  • ■
    Comprehensive SEO & Social Sharing Integration: Production-grade SEO setup including Schema.org JSON-LD structured data (Corporate Entity & Postal Address schema), automated dynamic XML sitemap (/sitemap.xml), robots.txt crawler directives, and branded OpenGraph preview cards for WhatsApp and social sharing.
  • ■
    Performance Optimization, SSL Encryption & Multi-Browser QA: Instantaneous page loading via server-side rendering, asset compression, SSL/TLS HTTPS encryption, and rigorous cross-browser testing on Google Chrome, Apple Safari, Microsoft Edge, and Firefox.
